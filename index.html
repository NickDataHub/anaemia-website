How to take a suitable photo
  1. Use even, natural light and avoid flash, shadows and filters.
  2. Gently pull down your lower eyelid and look upwards.
  3. Keep the exposed inner eyelid in focus and fill most of the frame with your eye. Avoid reflections or anything covering the eyelid.
  4. Check the highlighted region after analysis. If it misses the inner eyelid or includes other areas, disregard the result and try another photo.

The app cannot reliably check whether a photo is suitable.

Check the highlighted area

Experimental model prediction

This prediction is not a diagnosis. The model can miss anaemia or flag it incorrectly. Its cutoff is experimental and has not been independently validated. If you are concerned, ask a healthcare professional about a blood test.

Anaemia confidence score

A higher score means the model leans more towards anaemia. It is not the percentage chance of anaemia or the chance that the prediction is correct.

Selected eyelid region

The coloured area is the predicted eyelid region, not a map of disease or model attention. The full-image model also uses the surrounding image. If the selected region looks wrong, disregard the prediction.

How it works

A simple process.
A private experience.

01

Choose your photo

Use a clear, close-up photo of the inner lower eyelid. Lighting and image quality affect the result. The AI cannot check whether you have selected a suitable photo.

Our eyelid-region model was trained using project images and the Eye Conjunctiva Segmentation Dataset by Rahman and colleagues (2023), CC BY 4.0. Images and masks were resized for training.

02

Keep your image private

Your photo is analysed on your own device. It is not uploaded to a server, and we do not save your image or a history of your results.

03

Know your next step

Whatever the result, this tool cannot confirm or exclude anaemia. If you are concerned, a healthcare professional can assess you and arrange a blood test. Learn about anaemia and testing ↗
