BEHIND THE PREDICTION

Your research.
Made accessible.

01

The same trained network

ResNet-50 with two output classes, adapted from your model development notebook. Images are resized to 224 × 224 and normalized with the training pipeline’s ImageNet values.

02

Local by design

The model downloads once into the browser’s cache. Inference and attention visualization run on your device without a Python server.

03

Understand the limits

Lighting, image quality and differences from the training data can change predictions. The model does not verify that an image shows an eyelid. No clinical performance claim is made.
